# Piano samples

These fourteen files come from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by **Alexander Holm**, a
multisampled Yamaha C5, licensed **CC BY 3.0**.

- Original library: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Taken from the reduced MP3 set published with Tone.js at https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

## Changes made

- Only the files covering C3 to D#6 are kept, at one velocity layer.
- Each is cut from about sixteen seconds to three, with a short fade at the end. The app never plays
  more than the first two and a bit, so the rest was download and memory for nothing.
- Re-encoded at 96 kbps, stereo and sample rate unchanged.

The app pitches each sample by at most a semitone to reach the notes in between, since they sit a
minor third apart. Nothing else is altered.
